1

Founding Gym Status

The Wolf's Den is hereby recognized as Founding Gym #1 of the ForgeGrip platform. This designation is permanent and non-transferable.

2

ForgeGrip Platform Access

Core Benefit: The Wolf's Den receives full, unlimited access to the ForgeGrip platform — including all current features and all features added in the future — at no cost.
This access remains FREE for as long as ForgeGrip has at least one (1) other paying gym actively subscribed to the platform.
If ForgeGrip ever drops below one (1) other paying gym, The Wolf's Den may be asked to contribute a platform fee of no more than $499/month. This maximum rate is locked permanently — it will never increase regardless of future pricing changes to other customers.
This contribution is only applicable if Francisco is no longer able to support the gym in person due to relocation. If Francisco remains local and active, no fee will be charged regardless of how many other paying gyms exist on the platform.
3

Gym Membership — Francisco & Family

The Gym Owner agrees to provide unlimited gym membership for Francisco Meza and up to three (3) family members (four individuals total), for as long as this Agreement remains in effect and The Wolf's Den operates as a gym.

4

Referral Commission — Active Sales

If the Gym Owner actively sells and closes a new gym onto the ForgeGrip platform, the Gym Owner shall receive:

Gyms Closed (Cumulative)Commission RateDuration Per Gym
Gyms 1 through 1010% of monthly fee12 months
Gyms 11 through 2010% of monthly fee18 months
Gyms 21 and beyond10% of monthly fee24 months
"Closed" means the referred gym has signed an agreement with ForgeGrip and made at least one monthly payment.
Commission payments begin the month after the referred gym's first payment is received.
5

Referral Commission — Introductions Only

If the Gym Owner provides a warm introduction or referral lead and ForgeGrip independently closes the gym, the Gym Owner receives 5% of the referred gym's monthly platform fee for twelve (12) months.
A referral qualifies only if the Gym Owner provides the lead in writing (text or email) before ForgeGrip has made any prior contact with that gym.
Referral and active sales commissions are not stackable on the same gym. If the Gym Owner both refers and actively closes the same gym, only the Active Sales rate in Section 4 applies.
6

Founding Gym Recognition

The Wolf's Den will be recognized as Founding Gym #1 on forgegrip.app and in marketing materials, subject to the Gym Owner's approval of any specific language used.
A testimonial quote from the Gym Owner may be used in marketing materials with prior written approval. Text message or email constitutes written approval.
7

Term & Termination

This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues indefinitely unless terminated by mutual written agreement.
Either party may request to renegotiate terms with thirty (30) days written notice. Existing commission obligations for already-referred gyms survive termination for their full applicable term.
If The Wolf's Den permanently closes, this Agreement ends automatically. Accrued unpaid commissions shall be paid within 30 days.
8

General

This Agreement is between Francisco Meza and The Wolf's Den Gym Owner individually. It does not create a partnership, joint venture, or employment relationship.
Commission payments will be made via by the 15th of each month for the prior month's activity.
This Agreement may be amended only in writing, signed by both parties.
