// Know These Cold

5 things to
say out loud.

You don't need to memorize a script. Just know these five points well enough to say them naturally in a conversation.
01
The WOD goes live in 30 seconds.
Coach opens the app, builds the workout, hits publish. Every TV in the gym updates in real time. No printing, no whiteboard, no texting the workout to the front desk.
02
One tap. No app download. No password.
Members walk up to the kiosk screen, tap their name, and their streak and rank update instantly. Zero friction = higher check-in rate = athletes that feel seen.
03
The leaderboard runs itself.
Points accumulate automatically based on check-ins, PRs, and coach recognition. Members compete. Competitive athletes don't cancel. Retention solves itself.
04
Coaches see who's struggling before they quit.
The "Who Needs Help" screen shows which members have gone quiet and what skill areas are getting flagged. Proactive coaching. Not finding out someone quit after they're already gone.
05
Built for this sport — not adapted from something generic.
Warped Wall, Salmon Ladder, Cliffhanger, Rope Climb are first-class exercises in the library. Race calendar, obstacle tracking, Spartan band logging — it's built by someone who trains in these gyms. Wodify was built for CrossFit. ForgeGrip was built for this.
The close line — use this when they hesitate
"If it keeps just one member from canceling their membership each month, it pays for itself."
Let that land. Then ask for the meeting. Don't say anything else.

"The setup is done for you. Francisco handles the whole onboarding — you get on a call, he sets everything up, trains your coaches, and you're live within a week. You don't touch anything technical."
"We're a small gym, I don't think we can afford it."
"Plans start at $299/month. If it keeps just one member from canceling, it pays for itself. Most gyms see payback in the first month."

"Francisco can do a 20-minute live demo — you'll see the actual app running, not a mockup. He can also set up a sandbox for your coaches to play with for a week. I'll get that set up."
