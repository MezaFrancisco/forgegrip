02 What Members Actually See
Athlete Card

Everything They've
Already Earned, In One Place.

Rank title, streak, badges, Wall of Records held, latest competition result, and a glowing skill-area radar built from real training volume over the last 30 days — nothing new to log, just the data that already exists, finally given a screen worth checking.

Monthly Rank

Not Just a Number —
What Closes the Gap.

A rank on its own doesn't answer the two things members actually ask: am I improving, and who do I pass next. This screen opens with where someone stands this month, who's directly above and below them, and the real gap in points — then up to three concrete levers, each pulled from the actual points system, ordered by how much of the gap they'd close.
