FORGEGRIP MARKETING PACK
Created October 27, 2026

Eight PNG images using the approved cyan/lime FG identity.

WHAT TO USE
01 Brand overview: visual reference for the logo family.
02 Logo on dark: horizontal logo for dark website sections, slide decks and email graphics.
03 App icon / profile avatar: square source for app icon exports and social profile pictures. Keep the symbol centered when using circular crops.
04 Square social: launch announcement for square social feeds.
05 Portrait social: taller 4:5 campaign creative for social feeds.
06 Vertical story: 9:16 story, reel cover or static vertical ad. Add a clickable link sticker in the social app; the pictured button is part of the image.
07 Website hero: 16:9 banner for landing pages, presentations and landscape campaign placements. Keep the complete composition visible rather than cropping away the headline.
08 Company cover: wide 3:1 source artwork with room at left for a profile photo. Preview each platform's crop before publishing; different services use different cover sizes.

PALETTE TARGETS
Near-black #0A0A0A
Cyan #06B6D4
Lime #A3E635
Warm off-white #F0EAE0
Generated pixels may vary slightly from these target colors.

USAGE
Keep proportions intact. Leave clear space around the logo. Use lime as a small accent. Avoid stretching, recoloring, adding glow or placing text across the mark.
All delivered images have dark backgrounds. Transparent logo trials showed artifacts and are excluded.
These are raster PNG assets, not editable vector master files. For app-store submission, export the square source to the required app/platform dimensions. For large print or exact brand reproduction, commission/refine an outlined vector master.
The image-generation process can introduce small variations in logo geometry between compositions; use 02-logo-dark.png as the primary standalone logo reference in this pack.
